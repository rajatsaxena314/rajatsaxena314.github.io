Investigating the Emergence of Self-Organized Hierarchies in Neural Network–Driven Robotic Matter

Master’s Thesis | Rulands Group, Arnold Sommerfeld Center for Theoretical Physics, Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität | March 2025 - Present#

Mentor: Prof. Dr Steffen Rualnds & Onurcan Bektas#

  • Investigating the emergence of self-organised hierarchies in robotic systems governed by deep neural networks (DNNs) for decision-making.

  • Assembling and programming autonomous robots to perform collective tasks. Implementing multiple neural network architectures trained on multimodal sensory inputs.

  • Designing and simulating experiments to model and analyse collective behaviour and performance outcomes under different tasks.
